Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2025

Summary: Learning OS is a private desktop application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or documents to our servers. Everything stays on your device and your personal Google Drive.

1. What is Learning OS?

Learning OS is a desktop application built with Tauri (Rust + web technologies) designed to help users organize knowledge, generate AI-assisted lessons, and save documents to their own Google Drive account.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The application runs entirely on your local device. Specifically:

  • No account registration is required on our end.
  • No analytics or telemetry data is sent to any server.
  • No usage data, crash reports, or behavioral data is collected.

3. Google Account & OAuth

Learning OS integrates with Google services (Google Drive and Google Docs) to allow you to save your notes. When you connect your Google account:

  • We use the standard OAuth 2.0 protocol via Google's official authentication system.
  • Your Google credentials (username and password) are never visible to us — they are entered directly on Google's login page.
  • We request the following permissions:
    • drive.file — Create and manage files in your Google Drive that were created by this app.
    • documents — Create and edit Google Docs on your behalf.
  • Your OAuth access token is stored only on your local device and is never transmitted to us.
  • We do not access any existing files in your Drive except those created by Learning OS.

4. Data Storage

All data generated by Learning OS is stored in two places:

  • Your device: A local SQLite database on your computer stores your lessons, notes, and settings.
  • Your Google Drive: When you choose to save a document, it is uploaded directly to your personal Google Drive account.

We have no access to either of these storage locations.

5. Third-Party Services

Learning OS may use the following third-party services at your direction:

  • Google Drive API — to save documents to your Drive.
  • Google Docs API — to create formatted documents.
  • AI providers (if configured) — to generate lesson content. These requests are made directly from your device using API keys you provide.

Each of these services is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Learning OS is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the application, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
