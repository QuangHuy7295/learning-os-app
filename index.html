Explore Features Privacy Policy

What it does

Everything you need to learn effectively

Built for students and lifelong learners who want to capture and keep knowledge.

🤖

AI-Powered Lessons

Generate structured lessons from any topic with AI assistance and save them instantly to your personal library.

📄

Google Docs Integration

Automatically save your lessons as Google Docs in your Drive — organized, titled, and linked in one click.

🔒

Your Data, Your Drive

All documents are saved to your personal Google Drive account. We never store your content on our servers.

📚

Knowledge Library

Browse, search, and organize everything you've learned in a beautiful local library — always offline-first.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Built with Rust and Tauri for native performance. No Electron overhead, minimal memory footprint.

🛡️

Private by Design

Your Google credentials are stored only on your device. We request only the minimum necessary permissions.
